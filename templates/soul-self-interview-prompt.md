# SOUL self-interview — the paste-and-go prompt

*Send this to the person with one instruction: "Paste everything below the line into
Claude (or ChatGPT) and follow along. Takes about 40 minutes. Talk instead of typing —
press the 🎤 mic key and just answer out loud."*

*Works in claude.ai, the Claude app, ChatGPT, or a Claude Code session. When it's done
they send you the draft it produces; that draft feeds gate 07 of the install.*

---

You are about to interview me so a personal AI assistant can be set up that actually
knows me. Run it exactly like this:

**How to behave:**
- Ask **one question at a time** and wait for my answer. Never stack questions.
- After I answer, you may ask ONE short follow-up if my answer was vague or if I said
  something interesting in passing — the aside is usually better than the answer. Then
  move on.
- Push past vague. If I say "I want to be more organised," ask what being disorganised
  cost me *this week*.
- I might answer by voice dictation, so expect loose sentences. Never correct my
  phrasing — my exact words are the point. Quote me verbatim later.
- I can say **"skip"** (next question), **"skip section"**, or **"pause"** (you summarize
  where we are so I can resume later by pasting this again with your summary).
- First, ask me: is this assistant for **work + life** or **just life**? If just life,
  skip the WORK section entirely.

**The questions, in order:**

IDENTITY — 1. What do you actually do all day — not your title, where the hours go?
2. How would you describe your own working style, in your own phrase? 3. Where are you
and what's your timezone? 4. **If this assistant works perfectly, what does it do for
you? One sentence.** (Push me hard on this one — it's the most important question.)

MISSION — 5. What am I trying to make happen in the next few months, with a number and
date if there is one? 6. What's the one thing that, if it slipped, would matter most?
7. What have I already decided NOT to do? 8. What does everyone keep telling me to do
that I'm deliberately not doing?

WORK (skip if just-life) — 9. What do I sell and how does money actually work?
10. Where do things really stand — revenue, costs, runway? (Be blunt: an assistant that
thinks I'm flush will suggest expensive things.) 11. Who else is involved in the work?

VOICE — 12. When an assistant or app annoys me, what did it just do? 13. Long or short
answers? Answer first or reasoning first? 14. Do I want to be told when I'm wrong?
15. Casual or formal? 16. Should it acknowledge a message before it starts working?
17. Do I send voice notes, and do I want spoken replies back? (Two separate questions.)
18. Will it ever draft things I send to other people — and if so, what words or
punctuation do I ban?

RHYTHM — 19. When am I sharpest? 20. What does my day-shape look like? 21. When should
it absolutely not ping me? 22. Is there one habit that, if it slips, everything slips?
23. What helps when things are going badly? (So it can say "go for a walk" instead of
offering another tactic.)

HARD NOS — 24. What must it never, ever do — and what's the consequence that makes each
rule real?

PEOPLE — 25. Who will I mention that it needs to know instantly — name, relationship,
and the one thing that matters? 26. What have I learned the hard way, with the cost
attached? 27. What decisions are settled that I don't want reopened?

**When we finish (or I say "done"):**

Write **SOUL DRAFT — [my name] — [today's date]** as flowing prose: briefing notes for
a new chief of staff who starts Monday, NOT a Q&A dump. Quote my exact words where I
said something well. Mark anything I skipped or didn't know as `⟨ASK⟩` — never invent.
Date any fact that changes over time. Keep it under two pages.

Then tell me: **"Copy everything above and send it to Oz — that's the whole job."**
(If you are a Claude Code session with file access, also save it to
`~/soul-interview-draft.md`.)

Begin now with the work-or-life question, then question 1.
