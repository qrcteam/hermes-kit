## The problem it solves

In the customer's words (from the first written offer, see below):

- *"You have more moving parts than time."*
- Work built with AI vanishes — a chat disappears and takes a nearly-finished automation
  with it. *"Everything you have taught AI about you is sitting on one laptop."*
- They don't want to be the person building the CRM. They want to be in their curriculum,
  with their clients, on stages — **in their zone of genius**.

The solution: a personal AI assistant that lives on *their* machine, knows them, doesn't
forget, backs itself up, and connects to the tools they already pay for.

## The journey — six months, three phases (plus the fourth)

| Phase | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Initiate** | ~6 weeks | Install and connect. The map, Hermes sandboxed, the vault backing itself up, their models + Telegram wired in, their key system (CRM etc.) connected, one stuck project finished. Weekly working calls. |
| **2 · Investigate** | ~6 weeks | The map deepens. Opportunities surface. The AI learns the person and their business. Refining and tweaking what phase 1 installed. |
| **3 · Innovate** | ~3 months | Expanding creativity and automation in specific, personal ways — the system starts actively supporting the person's zone of genius rather than just remembering things. |
| **4 · _____ate** (ongoing) | month-to-month | Support, maintenance, improvement. Access to Oz during set working hours — **no 1-on-1 calls**. Latest prompts and processes; the memory and system kept performing at their best. |

**The retainer ruling (Oz, 2026-08-12):** two tiers, cleanly split by state, not by calendar:

- **$2,500/mo — full build, rock-and-roll retainer.** Scheduled calls, monthly objectives.
  This is the active-build price: the system is still being assembled and Oz is driving.
- **$500/mo — access & support.** Starts once a *stable place* is reached — major
  automations live, working assistant in place. Working-hours access, latest prompts and
  processes, **no 1-on-1 calls**. Capped at 25.

The trigger for the price drop is a **state** ("stable, major automations, working
assistant"), not a date — reaching it is the shared goal, and the reward for reaching it is
the bill going down 80%. That's an honest incentive and it should be said out loud in the
offer.

**Naming the fourth phase** — candidates, one will win:

- **Elevate** ← current favourite: says improvement, not just upkeep
- Cultivate — the gardening register; tends the system and the person
- Iterate — honest about what the work actually is; maybe too engineer-flavoured
- Accelerate — stronger promise, harder to keep

The $500 cap at a small group — **25 or fewer** — is part of the offer, not a supply
problem: it's what makes "access to me" true.

## The first written offer — Laurie, 2026-08-12

The offer got clear for the first time in an email. Reference copy:

- **Email:** "Here it is in writing" — oz@beautiful-possibilities.com →
  Laurie@dextropreneur.com, sent 2026-08-12 16:23 UTC
  (Gmail message `19ff6c9a67b62e72`, thread `19ff6b7c444e18f4`)
- **Price:** $1,500, one payment, up front — founder-cohort price, said plainly in the email
  ("you're one of five people I'm doing this with while I build it")
- **Market anchors named in it:** $10,000–15,000 for comparable installs; $5,000 quoted
  elsewhere for the mapping stage alone
- **Shape:** six weeks, six weekly calls (60–90 min), questions + voice notes between calls
- **Deliverables:** the map (built by Oz, not them) · Hermes installed & sandboxed · memory
  vault that backs itself up · their ChatGPT/Claude + Telegram wired in · their CRM
  (GoHighLevel) connected & readable · **one stuck project finished**
- **The "not included" list is load-bearing** — not unlimited time, not all five projects at
  once, not a CRM rebuild, not their subscriptions, not content written for them, no promised
  outcomes. *"I'd rather be boring about this now than awkward in week four."*
- **Phase 2 deliberately unpriced** at the six-week mark — *"Six weeks in we'll both know what
  you actually need and we'll price that then. Nothing renews on its own."*
- **Deadline:** reply by Monday the 17th; one slot held.

That email is the tone canon as much as the price canon: plain sentences, what-I-heard-you-say
first, the honest reason it's a system and not more hours.
