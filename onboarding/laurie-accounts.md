# Before we build your assistant — the accounts to create

Everything here takes about twenty minutes, and you only have to do the sign-up part. Don't
create keys, projects or anything clever inside them — we do that together on the call so
nothing lands in the wrong place.

**Use one email address for all of them** (whichever you consider your main business email),
and keep the passwords where you normally keep passwords.

---

## The six

### 1. Telegram — free
The app you'll actually text your assistant in. Install it on your phone and make an account
if you don't have one.

**Don't make the bot yet** — that's a two-minute job we do together on the call, on your phone.

### 2. A ChatGPT Plus **or** Claude Pro subscription — $20/month
This is the thinking part. It has to be **in your name, on your card** — it's your account and
your relationship with the model, not mine, and it's the only real monthly cost in the whole
setup.

If you already pay for one of them, that's the one we'll use. If you have neither and don't
care which: ChatGPT Plus is the smoother path.

### 3. GitHub — free
This is the backup. Your assistant's memory gets copied here every time it changes, privately,
so a dead laptop costs you a laptop and not everything you've taught it. Sign up, confirm the
email, stop there.

### 4. Pinecone — free
This is what lets you ask *"who did I hire for the bathroom?"* and have it find the note that
says *"went with Ruiz"* — searching by meaning instead of by exact words. The free tier is
genuinely enough.

Sign up here: <https://app.pinecone.io/?sessionType=signup>

### 5. OpenRouter — about $10, one time
The understudy. It runs small background jobs and covers you if your main model has a bad day.
Sign up and put $10 of credit on it; in practice it costs pennies a month.

<https://openrouter.ai>

### 6. Obsidian — free, **no account needed**
Just download and install it. It's the window you'll use to read your assistant's memory as
normal pages. Nothing to sign up for.

<https://obsidian.md>

---

## One you already have

**GoHighLevel** — bring your login. We'll connect it so the assistant can read what's in there
and answer questions about it. You'll need to be able to get to the settings area where API
keys live; if someone else set up your account, check now that you can.

---

## What to bring to the call

- Your phone, with Telegram installed and you logged in
- Your laptop, plugged in
- The six logins above, plus GoHighLevel
- Confirmation that your laptop is set to **not sleep** while plugged in — your assistant can
  only answer while the machine is awake

---

## What I'm not asking you for

No API keys, no tokens, no passwords sent to me. Every key gets created by you, on your screen,
during the call. You own all six accounts, and if you ever fired me tomorrow you'd keep the
whole thing working without me.
